Logistics & Supply Chain Performance Dashboard (Power BI)
Business Problem : DataCo processes thousands of global orders every year, but delayed deliveries reduce customer satisfaction, increase operational costs, and lead to significant revenue loss.

This dashboard helps the VP of Supply Chain identify delivery bottlenecks, evaluate shipping performance, and prioritize operational improvements.

Key Insights :
Total revenue reached $36.78M, generating $3.97M profit.
Only 45.18% of orders were delivered on time, while 54.82% experienced delays.
Delayed deliveries contributed to approximately $20.13M in lost sales.
Western Europe recorded the highest sales lost due to late deliveries (~$3.29M).
First Class shipping experienced the highest late-delivery rate, while Standard Class showed the best delivery performance.
Consumer customers generated the highest sales among customer segments.
Fishing products generated the highest sales and profit among product categories.
